CHAPTER 6 & 7 • ONBOARDING CHECKLIST

SISTAR Challenger Onboarding Checklist

This checklist guides you through your first week as a SISTAR Challenger — setting you up for confident, meaningful contribution from day one.

Phase 1

Pre-Arrival Checklist (Week Before)

  • Sign and return all documents (agreement, NDA, payment forms) via secure e-signature portal
  • Receive and read Welcome Packet & SISTAR Principals
  • Activate Google Workspace and SISTAR Web & App credentials
  • Test all system access and confirm 2FA setup
  • Receive Project Starter Pack & accept first-week calendar invites
  • Connect with your assigned Onboarding Buddy
Phase 2

First Day Schedule (Orientation)

  • 9:30 AM — HR Welcome Call: 30-min video call to confirm access and answer admin questions
  • 10:00 AM — Systems Setup: Log in to all accounts, set up profile and 2FA
  • 11:00 AM — 1-on-1 with Project Lead: 45-min deep dive into your role and priorities
  • 2:00 PM — Team Introduction: 30-min meeting to meet project teammates
  • 3:00 PM — Project Immersion: Review Project Starter Pack and shared documents
Phase 3

First Week Integration

  • 1. Meet Onboarding Buddy: 30-min chat on culture and workflows
  • 2. Get Your First Task: Well-defined "quick win" task to learn the process
  • 3. Shadow a Key Meeting: Sit in on daily stand-up or weekly sync
  • 4. Daily Check-ins: Brief 15-min daily syncs with Project Lead
  • 5. End-of-Week Wrap-up: Celebrate progress and set Week Two goals
CHAPTER 8 & 9 • INVITATION & MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT

Master Service Agreement Framework

A comprehensive legal framework for Independent Contractor Services between SISTAR Company ("Company") and the Challenger ("Independent Contractor"). All payment principles and calculations are explained in full before signing.

Section 1 – 3: Services, Compensation & Term

Services are detailed in jointly executed Statements of Work (SOW). Monthly invoices are remitted within 30 days. Valid for 6 months with an optional 6-month extension; 30 days' notice for convenience termination.

Section 4: Independent Contractor Status

The Challenger is an independent contractor — not an employee or agent. SISTAR controls final results, not the manner of work.

Section 5 & 6: Confidentiality & IP

All proprietary information and trade secrets must be held in strict confidence. All deliverables and source code created are "work made for hire" assigned to SISTAR Company.
