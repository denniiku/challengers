CHAPTER 2 • KEY ACTIVITIES BY DEPARTMENT

Core Responsibilities Across 10 Departments

A comprehensive organizational reference of core responsibilities across every SISTAR department.

1. Administration (Adm)

People Operations: Recruitment, sourcing, onboarding, compensation design, employee relations, and HRIS payroll management.
Development & Compliance: Training programs, labor law compliance, performance management, and health/safety standards.
Strategic Planning: Workforce/succession planning, high-performance culture, and knowledge transfer.

2. Finance (F'ce)

Core Financial Operations: Accounting, bookkeeping, AP/AR, FP&A forecasting, treasury/cash flow, and financial reporting.
Strategy, Controls & Analysis: Tax planning, capital budgeting, payroll compliance, risk management, internal auditing, investor relations, and COGS/P&L margin analysis.

3. Business Development (Dvmp.)

5-Stage Growth Engine: 1. Market Research → 2. Lead Generation → 3. Partnership Development (JVs, alliances) → 4. Pipeline Management → 5. Pitching & Closing, plus ongoing account management and KPI forecasting.

4. Branding

Strategy Foundation: Brand purpose, values, target audience, and positioning.
Visual & Verbal Identity: Logos, color palettes, typography, tagline, and brand guidelines.
Implementation & Experience: Cohesive digital presence, storytelling, and customer interaction standards.

5. Marketing

Research & Strategy Layer: Market Research & Marketing Strategy/Planning.
Content & Digital Layer: Content Creation, SEO/SEM, and Digital Marketing.
Social & Campaign Layer: Social Media, Email Marketing, PR & Outreach, Campaign Management, Analytics, and Brand Management.

6. Commercial / Sales Forces

5-Stage Revenue Pipeline: 1. Prospecting → 2. Discovery (product-market fit) → 3. Presentation → 4. Proposal (quotes, scope, pricing) → 5. Closing, followed by upselling, cross-selling, and quota forecasting.

7. Online Marketing

Search & Content: SEO (Google/Naver), PPC campaigns, blogs, videos, infographics, and Instagram/LinkedIn/YouTube management.
Email & Optimization: Lead nurturing automation, influencer/affiliate partnerships, Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO), Online PR, and Google Analytics.

8. CS & Supply Chain

End-to-End Logistics: 1. Procurement (supplier vetting, PO workflows) → 2. Inbound Operations (FIFO/LIFO inventory, real-time tracking) → 3. Fulfillment (picking, packing, outbound delivery) → 4. Operations & Analysis (warehousing, reverse logistics, freight auditing).

9. Regulatory Affairs (RA)

Compliance Guardian: Regulatory Strategy & Submissions (FDA, EMA technical dossiers), Compliance & Labeling review, Agency Communication & License Renewals, and Clinical Trials & Pharmacovigilance adverse event reporting.

10. Information Technology (IT)

Digital Infrastructure: Help Desk & Network (routers, firewalls, connectivity), Security & Infrastructure (cybersecurity policy, AWS/Azure/GCP cloud management, disaster recovery), and Data & Applications (databases, software lifecycle, IT governance).
